## The Why — Why This Works Better Than "Here's My Album"

- **5 tracks, not 50.** Lower commitment, higher completion. Fans actually finish.
- **Ranking mechanic.** Turns passive listening into active engagement. Fans invest = fans return.
- **No signup wall for first-listen.** Removes the biggest friction point. They fall in love first, sign up second.
- **Short enough to text.** `mystationlive.com/quickplay/vol-1` fits in one line. No awkward wrapping.
- **Vol number builds anticipation.** "Vol 3 coming?" Yes, but only if Vol 1 + 2 land. Scarcity + signal.
