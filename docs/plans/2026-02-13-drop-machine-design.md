# Drop Machine — IDMG Merch Strategy 2026

Timed merch drops every 2-4 weeks tied to music releases, events, and cultural moments. Three brands: IDMG, LOTL, MPF. Automated via THE MOB (12 elite agents).

## Marketing Channels
1. **Instagram** — Primary discovery (Reels, Stories, Posts)
2. **Email** — Highest conversion (sequences, announcements)
3. **SMS** — Urgency (flash sales, drop alerts)

## Drop Sequence Template
- D-7: Teaser (silhouette/blur)
- D-3: Reveal (full product + date)
- D-1: Countdown + email blast
- D-0: DROP IS LIVE (all channels)
- D+1: Social proof
- D+3: Last chance / low stock

## LOTL Festival Merch Plan
- **Pre-festival:** Early bird merch bundles, festival tees
- **On-site:** "I Was There" exclusives, numbered posters (500 max), festival-only colorways
- **Post-festival:** Recap collection, "I missed out" restocks
- **Promo tie-in:** $50+ merch = 25% off tickets, $100+ = 1 free ticket
