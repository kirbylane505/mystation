# 💰 REVENUE MODEL

## How MyStation Makes Money

### 1. Creator Subscriptions
| Tier | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Free** | $0 | Basic profile, 1 playlist, links only |
| **Starter** | $9.99/mo | Store (10 products), 5 playlists, basic analytics |
| **Pro** | $19.99/mo | Unlimited products, merch store, advanced analytics |
| **Business** | $49.99/mo | Custom domain, team access, priority support |

### 2. Transaction Fees
| Transaction | MyStation Cut |
|-------------|---------------|
| Product Sales | **8%** |
| Merch Sales | **10%** |
| Tips | **5%** |
| Subscriptions (fan-to-creator) | **10%** |

### 3. Listener Subscriptions
- **$4.99/mo** - Unlimited streaming on MyStation catalog
- Access to creator stations
- Ad-free experience

### 4. Advertising (Future)
- Promoted stations
- Sponsored playlists
- Banner ads on free tier
