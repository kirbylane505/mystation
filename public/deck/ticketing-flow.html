03 — Event Day

Gate Check-In Flow

📷

Scan QR Code

Gate staff opens mystationlive.com/admin/check-in on any phone or tablet. Point camera at the fan's QR ticket.

✅

Instant Verification

System verifies the HMAC signature in real-time. Shows green checkmark for valid tickets, red X for invalid or already used.

🔓

One-Time Use

Once scanned, ticket is marked as "used" in the database. Can't be scanned again — prevents duplication and fraud.

No special hardware needed. Works on any smartphone with a camera.
